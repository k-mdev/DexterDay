How to be Dexter

  • Roam the forest and grab treats. 🦴 🥓 🧀 🍗
  • Carried treats can be stolen. Drop them in your bowl by the porch to stash them.
  • Treats are also how Dexter heals: press Q to eat one. Eat it or stash it?
  • Now and then a rival dog snatches a treat if it catches you, or raids your bowl if you leave it unguarded. Battles are your call: walk up to a rival and press E to challenge them and win the loot back.
  • Battles: Slobber, Whine, Bark, or Duh. Eat a carried treat to recover, or sadly Bribe your way out with one.
  • Make friends: the chickens by the lake fetch treats, and down the street Hank the donkey kicks rivals into orbit, and Hamlet the pig roots up snacks. They tag along for a while, then head home.
  • Every two days the season changes and the trees and flowers regrow differently. The house and yard stay put.
  • Do not lick the cane toads.
  • Dig at dirt mounds, chase the squirrel (use Zoomies!), cool off in the lake or your kiddie pool.
  • When it gets dark, head up the porch steps to your bed to end the day. Rivals can't get onto the porch.
  • The DexDetector pings rival dogs as its sweep passes them. Yellow blips are carrying your treats.
